About GMIT College

Our mission is to provide a transformative, holistic education that fosters curiosity, critical thinking and global citizenship. GMIT College is a project of Global School, with 20 years of experience and the only institute in town offering postgraduate programs.

Flexible schedules with access to lectures and materials at your convenience.

🏅 Scholarships

Financial assistance to make education more accessible.

📚 Books & library

Textbooks, reference materials and research resources.
